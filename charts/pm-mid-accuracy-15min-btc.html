THE BAR TO BEAT
Confirmed after bug-fix: The 15-min PM mid has genuinely strong negative skill (−18.8% BSS). This is actually worse than V1 (−4.6%) because V1's pre-market 50/50 books were diluting the bad numbers. The mid is massively overconfident: when it says 4% → reality is 45%, when it says 96% → reality is 63%. Res/Rel = 0.09 — the mid barely separates outcomes and what separation exists is swamped by calibration error.
5-Min vs 15-Min: The 5-min mid has +1.5% BSS (slight positive skill). The 15-min mid has −18.8% BSS. The overconfidence problem scales dramatically with duration — 15 minutes gives the mid 3× more time to drift to extremes that don't reflect true probabilities. → See 5-min dashboard
